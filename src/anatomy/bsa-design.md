# Das Wurzelfenster — Mosteller body surface area

Figure size: a dedicated upright photographic masking frame, 47 solid parts.
This is an adjustable aperture, not another plotting table or a pressure bridge.

The initial opening has dimensionless width H/(60 cm) and height W/(60 kg).
Its area is H_cm × W_kg / 3600. A shape control turns that opening into an equal-area
square; the square's side is the numeric value of the repository's estimated BSA
in m². The opening is a mathematical construction, not a body outline. Both input
controls share the original form's raw values. The form control is explicitly a
visual explanation and cannot change the medical result.

## Parts before geometry

- Base: heavy rounded plinth, sole, four leveling pads, four feet, perimeter
  groove and four screw rings/centers.
- Stand: two footplates, four bolt pads, two upright stiles, lower crossbar,
  upper tie bar, two top caps, front and rear guide strips.
- Aperture: fixed left jaw, fixed bottom jaw, bottom riser, variable right jaw,
  variable top jaw, luminous opening, rooted scale ticks.
- Right drive: rear horizontal guide, slider body, slider cap, rear connection
  tongue, spindle pedestal and knurled crown.
- Top drive: front vertical guide, lift carriage, carriage cap, spindle boss and
  thumbwheel; front and rear carriage layers stay separate even at closure.
- Controls: base controller body, lid, knob base and knob crown, screw rings,
  closed vent slots, calibration plaque and ruled strips.

Painter order is solved from the union of measured overlaps at every audited
pose, including all four height/weight limit combinations. A cycle or failed
audit prevents replacement of generated art.

World origin of the opening is x = −100, z = 40, y = −1..0. The top and right
jaws meet on flush faces. The top carriage rides the front of the left stile;
the right carriage rides a rear rail, so they cannot collide near a closed
opening. Fixed parts are prebuilt, rigid moving parts translate, and only the
opening and two variable-length jaws rebuild their paths. Every solid records
its real audit shape. One scale serves both dimensions; it stays fixed for
ordinary examples and fits the aperture to 180 world units for larger inputs.
